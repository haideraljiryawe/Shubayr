**Every day**

- ☐ Call every customer before confirming their order, and write the call note.
- ☐ Change a delivery fee at confirmation only with a clear reason.
- ☐ Check "Out of stock" and "Low stock" products in the product list.
- ☐ New stock from a supplier: **Restock** on the product (never opening stock), only the lines you received.
- ☐ Record the cash each driver hands in under **Cash receipts**.
