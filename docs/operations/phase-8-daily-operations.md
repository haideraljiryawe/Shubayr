# Phase 8 daily operations

This is the staff routine for a normal trading day. All dates shown or entered
by staff are Baghdad business dates. Keep the operation ID generated by the
client when retrying a timed-out request; reusing it with the same request is
safe, while changing the request under the same ID is an error.

## 1. Prepare and dispatch orders

1. Open the order queue and check the customer, address, payment method, and
   any accepted price change (`orders.view`).
2. Accept the order, prepare it, pick the stock, and mark it ready
   (`orders.accept`, `orders.prepare`, `inventory.pick`, and
   `orders.mark_ready`). Weight items must use the picked decimal quantity;
   do not round them to a whole unit.
3. For an internal agent, choose the agent and assign the delivery
   (`orders.assign_agent`). Handover the ready order only after the goods have
   physically changed hands (`orders.handover`).
4. Check the party custody screen after handover (`deliveries.manage`). Staff
   need `cost.view` as well to see and reconcile goods at cost.

## 2. Record an internal-agent delivery

The signed-in delivery agent sees only their assigned work and custody. The
mobile role is `delivery_agent`; it does not require an admin permission.

- Mark the delivery out for delivery when leaving with it.
- Record the actual delivered quantities and collected cash. A short delivery
  must contain the real delivered quantity, not the original order quantity.
- If the agent cannot confirm the collection, leave it unconfirmed. Staff list
  these items with `deliveries.manage` and confirm the collection only after
  checking the physical cash (`orders.deliver`).
- A staff member can record or correct operational status using `orders.retry`
  for a new attempt, `orders.fail` for a failed attempt, and `orders.deliver`
  for a completed delivery.

Never turn a failed delivery directly into a stock adjustment. Either retry it
or open a retrieval so the custody trail remains complete.

## 3. Run an external-driver trip

1. Create and maintain the driver record (`drivers.manage`). External drivers
   do not sign in to the app.
2. Create the trip, add ready orders, and start it only at physical handover
   (`trips.manage`). Use `customer_direct` when the customer pays the driver
   fare directly; this keeps the store collection and driver fare separate.
3. Review the live trip and its orders (`trips.view`). Record deliveries,
   failures, retries, collections, and exceptions through the same staff
   delivery and exception screens used for internal agents.
4. At return, count the cash and goods with the driver. A different authorised
   staff member closes the trip (`trips.settle`). Enter the counted amounts,
   even when there is a difference. The close screen must continue to show any
   short cash, excess cash, unpaid fare, or unretrieved goods until separately
   resolved.

## 4. Receive and allocate cash

1. Compare the party collection list and custody statement with the cash being
   handed over (`deliveries.manage`).
2. Record one cash receipt for the physical handover
   (`cash_receipts.receive`). Select the actual cash account; viewing account
   details requires `cash_accounts.view`.
3. Allocate the receipt to one or several confirmed order collections
   (`cash_receipts.allocate`). It is valid to leave a remainder and allocate it
   later. The unallocated receipt queue is available with
   `deliveries.manage`.
4. Never edit a posted voucher. If the handover was recorded incorrectly, a
   second authorised user reverses it (`cash_receipts.reverse`) and staff enter
   the correct voucher with a new operation ID. Separation-of-duties rules are
   enforced by the service.

## 5. Resolve delivery exceptions

Review the exception list and details with `custody_exceptions.view`.

- Lost or damaged goods: record the actual quantity and whether the store or
  delivery party bears the loss (`custody_exceptions.loss`).
- Customer refusal at the door: record the uncollected return, then receive it
  into the named warehouse through the retrieval workflow
  (`custody_exceptions.return_uncollected`, `retrieval.open`,
  `retrieval.view`, and `retrieval.receive`).
- Delivery-fee refund: verify that the order was charged a fee, then settle the
  refund from the chosen IQD cash account or against still-uncollected cash
  (`custody_exceptions.refund_delivery_fee`; account lookup also needs
  `cash_accounts.view`).
- Reverse a wrong exception instead of editing it
  (`custody_exceptions.reverse`). A different user may be required by the
  configured separation-of-duties level.

## 6. End-of-day checks

Do these checks after all physical handovers have been entered and before any
period close.

1. Run the trial balance (`ledger.view`). Total debits must equal total credits,
   and the ledger must report balanced.
2. Compare warehouse lot value with inventory account 1000
   (`inventory.view`, `cost.view`, and `ledger.view`).
3. Compare the sum of all party goods at cost with goods-in-custody account
   1010, and party cash held with cash-in-custody account 1020
   (`deliveries.manage`, `cost.view`, and `ledger.view`).
4. Compare supplier balances with AP control account 2000
   (`suppliers.view` and `ledger.view`).
5. Review the unallocated cash-receipt queue against the remainder on every
   active voucher (`deliveries.manage`). A remainder is an allocation
   subledger balance; it does not currently have a separate GL account.
6. Check that each document series is sequential and investigate any apparent
   gap before closing. Replaying the original request with its original
   operation ID must return the same document and must not change balances.
7. Close an accounting period only as part of the agreed period-close process,
   not merely because the daily checks are complete (`period.close`).

Do not conceal a difference with a manual stock or journal workaround. Leave
the operational item open, keep the physical count, and escalate it with the
document number and operation ID.
