# Product decisions — 2026-10-17

## Agent wages and build phase 10

Agent wages are not part of the Shubayr application release. They belong to
the future delivery add-on together with the deferred delivery-agent
application.

Build phase 10 is therefore skipped. Do not implement phase-10 agent-wage
features in Shubayr, and do not remove or rewrite existing delivery, custody or
accounting functionality on its behalf. A future delivery add-on will define
its own wage scope and integration contract when that product decision is
made.
