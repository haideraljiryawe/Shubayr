# Product decisions — 2026-10-12

These decisions define the delivery and pricing scope for the first production release.

## Delivery application scope

Shubayr does not have a delivery-agent application. Delivery-agent application features are deferred as future add-ons. Current delivery work must support staff-operated fulfilment only; it must not introduce new delivery-agent application functionality.

## Web Admin launch

The Web Admin is scheduled to enter production in eight days so staff can upload and maintain the launch catalog. Delivery-zone and discount administration must therefore use the existing Web Admin/API authorization and auditing patterns and remain safe for that launch.

## Delivery pricing

Delivery fees are determined by governorate delivery zones. Each served governorate belongs to at most one active zone, and checkout snapshots that zone's fee on the order.

Staff always phone the customer while confirming an order. Confirmation records that contact and a short note. Authorized staff may adjust the order's delivery fee during confirmation, with a reason; the customer is notified of the resulting total and does not perform a separate acceptance step.

## Automatic order discount

Shubayr uses owner-managed tiered automatic discounts. The highest qualifying active tier applies to the goods subtotal. Automatic discounts do not apply to delivery fees and, by default, do not stack with coupons: the customer receives whichever discount is larger. The selected discount is snapshotted and allocated across order lines in whole IQD so later returns use the exact net line shares.
